Who We Are

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Impact Without Boundaries

We envision a world where funded research and innovation generates impact far beyond its immediate boundaries — where every project leaves a traceable legacy, and every partner's contribution is recognised, amplified, and remembered.

We are committed to continuous learning and technological evolution, ensuring that our methods remain as dynamic as the landscape we operate in. We monitor emerging platforms, adopt new methodologies, and integrate relevant technologies — so our partners always benefit from what's current, without having to track it themselves.

Our Mission

More Than Project Communication

We don't just communicate projects — we build the visibility infrastructure that lets each consortium member demonstrate their expertise, strengthen their institutional reputation, and grow their influence, project after project.

By embedding directly into consortia as a dedicated dissemination partner, we ensure that every partner's role, contribution, and knowledge is strategically communicated to the audiences that matter — peers, policymakers, industry, and society at large.

🔭

Continuous Innovation

We actively monitor emerging communication technologies and platforms to keep our methods ahead of the curve.

📈

Partner Visibility

Every consortium member's expertise and contribution is individually amplified — not just the project as a whole.

🌐

Global Reach

Our dissemination strategies transcend regional boundaries, targeting the stakeholders and audiences that drive real-world progress.

Our Expertise

Integrated EU Project Services

From consortium-embedded dissemination to full-cycle proposal development, SIA Agency delivers specialised expertise across every dimension of the EU funding lifecycle.

📡

Dissemination & Communication

Our core competency. We integrate into your consortium as a dedicated dissemination partner — architecting, executing, and fully automating your multi-channel communication strategy. Your partners contribute to dissemination without ever having to manually track metrics, calculate reach, or compile reporting tables. We handle it all.

  • Consortium dissemination work package leadership
  • Multi-channel outreach: LinkedIn, X, YouTube, CORDIS, newsletters, conferences & scientific publications
  • Automated reach & engagement tracking across all channels — no manual counting for partners
  • EC-compliant dissemination reports generated automatically, ready for submission
  • Stakeholder mapping, targeting & engagement frameworks
🎨

Visual Communication & Content Strategy

We translate complex research findings and technical innovation into compelling visual narratives that resonate with diverse audiences — from policymakers to the general public.

  • Data visualisation and scientific infographics
  • Project identity, branding & digital presence
  • Multimedia content production for all channels
  • Promotional materials aligned with EU visibility guidelines
📝

Proposal Development & Writing

Collaborating with seasoned EU funding specialists, we co-develop high-impact proposals that articulate scientific excellence, innovation potential, and societal relevance with precision and strategic clarity.

  • Project conceptualisation & theory of change design
  • Technical narrative writing for Horizon Europe and beyond
  • Budget architecture and financial compliance
  • Peer review coordination and submission management
🚀

Exploitation & Knowledge Transfer

Beyond dissemination, we help consortia translate project outcomes into lasting institutional and societal value — ensuring your results are adopted, replicated, and built upon well beyond the project lifecycle.

  • Exploitation strategy design & roadmapping
  • Policy brief development and uptake pathways
  • Open access and knowledge management compliance
  • Post-project sustainability planning & legacy frameworks
